# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful
- Use inclusive language
- Accept constructive feedback
- Focus on collaboration

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Trolling or insulting comments
- Public or private abuse

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove contributions or block users who violate these rules.
